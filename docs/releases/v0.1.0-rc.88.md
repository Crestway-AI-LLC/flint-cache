## What you will notice

- **A backup schedule that stops working can be alerted on** (ADR-0011's
  D8 amendment). The schedule used to record its failures, and the age of
  the newest set it had proven restorable, only in a status file nothing
  read. Name a status port in the inventory, `backup-listen <host:port>`, and
  `flint-backup schedule` answers `PING` and `FLINTINFO` on it, read-only.
  `restorable_overdue` is `1` when the newest set the strongest check proved
  (a rehearsal, else a verify, else the backup completing) is older than
  `restorable_bound_s`, which the seat computes from its own cadence.
  `flint-exporter --backup <host:port>` publishes it as
  `flint_backup_restorable_overdue`, the field to alert on. A job's failure
  streak is journaled once at its start, `BackupJobFailed` with the error,
  and once at its end, `BackupJobRecovered`.

- **A control-plane seat whose store cannot be written ends itself**
  (BUG-0282). On a three-seat control plane, a seat that was leader when a
  write to its own store failed (a full or read-only volume) went on
  answering as the leader, and could keep renewing the write lease of a
  master the real leader had fenced. It now exits, status 70, saying why,
  and the supervisor starts it again from what its store holds.

## What you must do

- **Upgrading from rc.87:** nothing. To alert on an overdue backup, add
  `backup-listen` with the backup host's address (not a wildcard: it is
  dialled), and point your exporter at it.

## Rolling back

- **To rc.87:** remove any `backup-listen` line from the inventory first.
  An rc.87 `flintctl` refuses a key it does not know.
